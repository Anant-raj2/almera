## 10/22/2025 - Keyboard Research and Preliminary Sketch  

Initially when I wanted to build my own ergonomic split keyboard I was aware of some features that I could potentially want however I had no concrete plan. I decided to allocate some time to look at other popular ergonomic keyboards from the community to consolidate a set of features that would be most beneficial for me.

# Market Research
I started out by looking at the klor. This was already pretty darn close to the ideal keyboard I was thinking about; however, there was an oled which I did not and it was a wired keyboard. I knew that I wanted my keyboard to be completely wireless.
![image.png](https://blueprint.hackclub.com/user-attachments/blobs/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6NDMxMywicHVyIjoiYmxvYl9pZCJ9fQ==--8c293da396052579be8f9e020465583a49c00ebd/image.png)

Moving on, I looked at the Goose and the Avocado which were MX styled keyboards with a slight splay and rotary encoders. I really appreciated the use of the XIAO BLE to make the goose completely bluetooth enabled. I also really like the rotary encoders that were used in the goose. Something that I believed could be improved is the first thumb key in the thumb arc. It is in a very awkward position and requires the thumb to stretch beyond what is comfortable to me. I also really didn't like the splay as I think it should be a little more intense.
![image.png](https://blueprint.hackclub.com/user-attachments/blobs/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6NDMxNCwicHVyIjoiYmxvYl9pZCJ9fQ==--64aebddcb764df491781a4e7232732be6cb021e8/image.png)

I then proceeded to look at other keyboards from the r/ErgoMechKeyboards community where I came across the Splay break. I really like the singlar key on the rightmost coloumn as it perfectly suited my needs. However I wanted more thumb keys with intense splays on them.
![image.png](https://blueprint.hackclub.com/user-attachments/blobs/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6NDMxNSwicHVyIjoiYmxvYl9pZCJ9fQ==--fcc98568612fa3d04f0abbed5bccaf967ae70209/image.png)

After browsing around a little more I came across the elder brother of the KLOR, the Totem. The difference was that this was a choc style keyboard - which wasn't to my liking. Nevertheless, looking past the style of the switches everything about the layout of this board was perfect. It was an ortholinear column staggered-splayed layout that had the perfect splay. It also had the singular key on the rightmost coloumn from the splaybreak and it had three thumb keys with a good splay. The only changes that I wanted to make to this was making it MX switches and including a rotary encoder.
![image.png](https://blueprint.hackclub.com/user-attachments/blobs/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6NDMzMSwicHVyIjoiYmxvYl9pZCJ9fQ==--44caff8e9a3617c573b803bbc3304d597f90d273/image.png)

# Preliminary Design
After doing some extensive market research as detailed above, I came up with the following desing matrix:
* Ortholinear
* Column-staggered
* Ring and pinky finger splay
* Thumb arc with three keys
* Rotary Encoder
* Wireless capable with the XIAO BLE
* 3x5 layout with the singular key on the rightmost side
After defining my criteria I came up with an initial sketch for what my keyboard would potentially look like.
